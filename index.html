RCSL Working Group “Histories of the Sociology of Law”

Approved as a Working Group of the Research Committee on Sociology of Law (RCSL) at the Board meeting in Bangor, Wales, 4 September 2024.

About the Working Group

The Working Group brings together scholars researching the intellectual, institutional, and biographical history of the sociology of law as a field.
